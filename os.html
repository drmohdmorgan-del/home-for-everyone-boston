Our system

The brokerage, run as a system.

Most brokerages wing it. We run six modules — presence, leads, listing launches, outreach, niche expertise, and reputation — so every client gets the same standard of care, every time.

01

Presence

This website, an optimized Google Business Profile, portal profiles, and LinkedIn — so clients can find us, check us, and trust us before the first call.

02

Lead engine

Buyer consultation and seller home-valuation funnels, listing alerts for serious searchers, and a loss-mitigation funnel for distressed homeowners who need a specialist, not a sales pitch.

03

Listing marketing OS

Every listing is launched, not listed: professional photography and video, a printed flyer, and a coordinated social rollout. The full treatment — not an afterthought.

04

Outreach OS

A real cadence with our sphere and past clients, attention to expired and cancelled listings, FSBO conversations, and an investor buyer list for the Boston–Worcester corridor.

05

Niche OS

Salustia Ortiz is a Certified Loss Mitigation Specialist — running a pre-foreclosure and short-sale pipeline with attorney and CPA referral partners. Her membership in the Women's Business Enterprise National Council and the Greater New England Minority Supplier Development Council opens supplier-diversity and corporate-relocation doors.

06

Review + content engine

A Google review requested after every closing, plus weekly market updates and just-sold content — so the track record builds itself in public.
